Using "IaC" to mean "Terraform" defines the whole practice by a single implementation. It also conveniently lets a vendor sell convergence as a new category.

Configuration management was converging machines on declared state long before Terraform. The term "infrastructure as code" came out of the Puppet and Chef community to describe it.

Wherever the config lives, something declares it, something converges it, and the two drift apart. Declaration, convergence and drift are IaC.

A real improvement to IaC can be sold on its own merit. "We don't do IaC" is aimed at buyers who think IaC means Terraform.
